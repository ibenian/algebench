# in the last 30 days. Compromised releases are normally caught and yanked
# within hours to days, so the cooldown removes most of that exposure at no
# real cost. A published CVE is the exception — its fix is usually days old,
# and waiting is the riskier choice — so `--security` bypasses the cooldown
# and says so loudly. The cooldown is never applied as a blanket re-resolve:
# rolling every package back raised this project's known-advisory
# count from 9 to 33.
